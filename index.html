The Nokia 3310

The phone that just wouldn't quit.

The Nokia 3310 is an iconic mobile phone that was released in 2000. It is known for its simple design, durability, and long battery life.
Its main features include calling and SMS. The reliability of this phone made it a global standard in the early 2000's

Key Features

  • Removable battery
  • Loud and clear audio
  • Physical keyboard
  • LCD display
Specification Details
Weight 133g
Dimensions 113 x 48 x 22mm
Battery life 260 hours standby 4.5 hours talk time
Storage 250 contacts

How to use

  1. Insert SIM card into back of the phone
  2. Charge the phone with included charger
  3. Turn on the phone using top power button
  4. Use keypad to make calls or send SMS messages
